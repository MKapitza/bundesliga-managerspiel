Bayern Munich, Borussia Dortmund, RB Leipzig and VfB Stuttgart confirm UEFA Champions League squads
04.09.2026
Summary
Bayern Munich, Borussia Dortmund, RB Leipzig and VfB Stuttgart have announced their squads for the UEFA Champions League league phase, which kicks off on Tuesday, 8 September.
Clubs can name an A list of up to 25 players, eight of whom must be 'locally trained'. Of those eight, a maximum of four can have spent at least three seasons under contract at another German club between the ages of 15 and 21, while the remainder must have spent those seasons with their current club.
There is also a B list for players born after 1 January 2005. As such,
Lennart Karl
has been named on that list rather than the A list and will still be eligible to compete for
Bayern
this term. The same applies to
Antonio Nusa
and
Assan Ouédraogo
at Leipzig.
The Bavarians will kick off their continental campaign on Thursday when they host Bodø/Glimt, while
RB Leipzig
travel to Como.
Dortmund
are in action against Villarreal on Tuesday, with
Stuttgart
facing Viking on Wednesday.
Michael Olise and Luis Díaz (l-r.) are among the established names in Bayern Munich's squad.
- IMAGO/Ulrich Hufnagel
